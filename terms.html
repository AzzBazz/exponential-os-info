Performance reporting

Search Console information can arrive late and can omit privacy-filtered searches. Reported average position is an aggregate, and individual search results can differ. Reports and rule-based opportunities are inputs for review; they do not guarantee rankings, traffic or business results.

Availability and updates

Automatic reporting depends on the local OS services running, an internet connection and valid Google access. Google's restrictions, revoked permissions or service interruptions can pause updates. The dashboard shows the last successful sync and retains the last saved report after a failed update.

The account holder can revoke Google access through Google Account connections. To request removal of locally held records or ask about authorised application access, contact Aaron Bennett at aaron.bennett5544@gmail.com.
