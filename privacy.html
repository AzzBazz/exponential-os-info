Privacy information

Exponential OS is a private application operated by Aaron Bennett on his Windows computer. This page explains the Google Search Console connection and this public information website.

Google data the app accesses

After the account holder grants Google's read-only Search Console permission, the app reads the list of available website properties and search-performance reports. Reports include dates, clicks, impressions, click-through rates, average positions, search queries, pages, countries, device types and search appearances. The current integration selects only the authorised AW Pressure Washing property for reporting.

Why the app uses this data

The data supports the local search dashboard, performance trends and rule-based opportunities for review. The connector does not change Search Console properties, submit URLs, manage website users or edit the website. It does not sell Google data, use it for advertising or send it to an AI provider.

Storage and sharing

Performance reports and their history are stored in the local OS database. Google OAuth connection credentials, including the refresh token, are stored separately and encrypted using Windows DPAPI for the signed-in Windows account. The app does not store Google passwords. Report records are not encrypted by this connector and are protected by the computer's local access controls.

The connector exchanges authentication and report requests directly with Google's services over HTTPS. Its local services transfer reports through the computer's loopback interface. Reports and tokens are not published on this information website. Other software and people with access to the Windows account may be able to access local records.

Retention and revocation

The private OS retains saved report history until its operator removes it. The account holder can revoke the app's Google access at any time in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorised retrieval once any existing access token expires; it does not delete previously saved reports. For removal of local reports or connection credentials, contact the operator below.

Google Limited Use

Exponential OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This information website

These are static information pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, registration forms or embedded OS dashboard. The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and access times, to deliver and secure the website. Links to Google follow Google's own privacy practices.
